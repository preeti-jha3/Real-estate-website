Help and FAQs

Quick answers before you call us
Are the listings verified?

Every listing is checked for ownership documents and a site visit before it goes live. Look for the Clear title tag on plots.

Do you charge brokerage?

Rentals have zero brokerage. On purchases, our advisor fee is shared upfront, so there are no surprises later.

How do I book a site visit?

Open any home, fill in your name and mobile number, and we call you within a day to fix a time that suits you.

How accurate is the EMI calculator?

It gives a close estimate using the standard reducing-balance formula. Your bank's final rate and processing fees may differ.

What is the security deposit on rentals?

Usually two months of rent, refundable when you move out. Exact terms are on each listing.

Can I save homes and see them later?

Yes. Tap the heart on any card. Saved homes stay on this device and show under Saved only.
